About Grupo-360

Who They Are?

• Origin: Culiacán, Sinaloa, Mexico • Formed: Around 2009-2010 • Original Members: David Loya, Pedro Villa, Andrés Vargas, and drummer José Barraza Hernández • Musical Style: Combines traditional norteño and corridos with rap elements (a hybrid style sometimes referred to as "rapteño"). • Notable Albums: La Escuela de Damaso (2011), Dos Jóvenes Muchachos (2014), and Seguimos En El Juego (2015). • Popular Songs: "Seguimos Laborando," "La Barda," "La Purga," "El Boom," and "Aquilesorganigrama".

Their Music

Grupo-360 represents a new generation of regional Mexican music, blending the rich traditions of the genre with modern influences to create a sound that bridges generations. From high-energy tracks that bring the party to heartfelt songs that tell personal stories, their music captures the diversity, passion, and culture of contemporary Mexican music. With a style that honors the roots of regional Mexican while embracing its evolution, Grupo-360 continues to connect with longtime fans and a new generation of listeners.

Their Journey

Through consistent releases, collaborations, and performances, Grupo-360 has expanded its audience beyond Mexico and developed an international following. Their growing presence demonstrates how regional Mexican music continues to evolve while maintaining its cultural roots. Grupo-360 continues to create music that reflects their identity and connects with fans through every release and performance.

More Than Music

For Grupo-360, music is also a way to represent culture, experiences, and the people who support them. Their identity is built around authenticity, creativity, and a connection with their audience. As their journey continues, Grupo-360 continues to explore new sounds while staying connected to the musical traditions that influenced them.

Grupo-360 — music with identity, culture, and its own mark.

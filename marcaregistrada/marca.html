About Marca Registrada

Who They Are?

Formed in 2014 in Culiacán, Sinaloa, Mexico, this popular Regional Mexican band is known for its modern blend of norteño, corridos, and sierreño music. They gained recognition with their 2015 debut album La Vida Ruina, featuring the late Ariel Camacho. Since then, they have achieved international success with hits like “Di Que Sí” with Grupo Frontera and “El Rescate” with Junior H. They have also collaborated with artists such as Carín León, Fuerza Regida, Maluma, and Julieta Venegas. Their 2023 album Don't Stop the Magic reached the US Billboard 200, followed by popular releases like The Goat and Evo.

Music

Marca Registrada represents a new generation of regional Mexican music. Their sound draws from established Mexican musical traditions while incorporating modern influences, creating songs that connect with both longtime fans of the genre and younger audiences. From high-energy tracks to more personal and emotional songs, their catalog reflects different sides of contemporary Mexican music and culture.

Journey

Through consistent releases, collaborations, and performances, Marca Registrada has expanded its audience beyond Mexico and developed an international following. Their growing presence demonstrates how regional Mexican music continues to evolve while maintaining its cultural roots. Marca Registrada continues to create music that reflects their identity and connects with fans through every release and performance.

More Than Just Music

For Marca Registrada, music is also a way to represent culture, experiences, and the people who support them. Their identity is built around authenticity, creativity, and a connection with their audience. As their journey continues, Marca Registrada continues to explore new sounds while staying connected to the musical traditions that influenced them.

Marca Registrada — music with identity, culture, and its own mark.
